# 6　大問別演習②（大問4形式・大問5形式）

## 大問4形式の解き方（30点・15分）

:::box 手順
1. 会話文は【1】〜【4】に分かれ、それぞれのあとに設問がある。**【1】を読んだら【1】の設問を解く**、の順で進める（全部読んでから解かない）。
2. **適文補充**は、空所の**直前の発言と直後の発言**を見る。質問→答え、提案→返事、のペアを探す。
3. **指示語（it / that / this / they）**は、直前の文の名詞や内容を指す。当てはめて意味が通るか確認。
4. **英作文（2文以上）**は、会話の流れに合わせて「自分の意見＋理由」。難しい単語を使わない。
:::

## R4-1　大問4形式 演習1

:::dialog 次は、中学生の Hana と、ALTの Mr. Davis の会話です。これを読んで、あとの問いに答えなさい。
**【1】**
Hana: Hello, Mr. Davis. Do you have time now?
Mr. Davis: Sure, Hana. What's up?
Hana: Our class is going to make a poster about our town for the English event next month. I want to introduce our town's summer festival, but I don't know how to start.
Mr. Davis: That sounds like a good topic. ( ① )
Hana: It is held every August. About ten thousand people come to see the fireworks.
Mr. Davis: Ten thousand! That's a lot. Have you ever been to a festival in another country?
Hana: No, I haven't. Are there festivals like this in your country?
:::

:::q 問1　（①）に入る最も適切な文をア〜エから選びなさい。【3点】
ア When is the festival held?　イ Why don't you go to the festival?　ウ How many posters do you need?　エ Who made the poster?
:::

:::dialog
**【2】**
Mr. Davis: Yes. In my hometown in Australia, we have a big festival in January. It's summer there in January.
Hana: Summer in January? That's interesting!
Mr. Davis: People enjoy music and food outside. ②It is very popular with families. Many people bring their own chairs and sit on the grass.
Hana: In Japan, many people wear *yukata* at summer festivals. Do people wear special clothes at your festival?
Mr. Davis: No, they don't. They wear T-shirts and shorts because it's very hot. ( ③ ) I think that's a nice difference.
:::

:::q 問2　下線部②の It が指すものを、本文中から英語3語で抜き出しなさい。【3点】

問3　（③）に入る最も適切な文をア〜エから選びなさい。【3点】
ア I have a yukata, too.　イ I didn't know that Japanese people wear yukata.　ウ Wearing yukata sounds beautiful.　エ It is cold in Australia in January.
:::

:::dialog
**【3】**
Hana: I want to put some pictures on the poster. ④( どの写真を使えばよいかわかりません。 )
Mr. Davis: Well, a picture of the fireworks is a must. And a picture of people wearing yukata will be good, too. People from other countries want to see how Japanese people enjoy the festival.
Hana: I see. Then I'll ask my father. He takes a lot of pictures every year.
Mr. Davis: Good. Also, write short sentences. People don't read long sentences on a poster.
Hana: OK. I'll write about the date, the place, and the fireworks.
:::

:::q 問4　下線部④の日本語を、（　）内の語を並べかえて英語にしなさい。 I don't know ( to / which / use / pictures ).【4点】

問5　Mr. Davis がポスターについてハナに助言したことを、日本語で2つ書きなさい。【各2点＝4点】
:::

:::dialog
**【4】**
Mr. Davis: By the way, why did you choose the festival for your poster?
Hana: Because it is my favorite event. When I was small, my grandmother took me there every year. ⑤I want people from other countries to know about it.
Mr. Davis: That's a great reason. Please tell that story on the poster, too.
Hana: I'll try. Thank you very much, Mr. Davis. ( ⑥ )
Mr. Davis: Of course. Come and show it to me anytime.
:::

:::q 問6　下線部⑤を日本語にしなさい。【3点】

問7　（⑥）に入る最も適切な文をア〜エから選びなさい。【3点】
ア Can I show you the poster when I finish it?　イ Did you go to the festival with me?　ウ Shall I make a poster for you?　エ May I go to Australia with you?

問8　本文の内容と合うものをア〜エから1つ選びなさい。【3点】
ア Hana has been to a festival in Australia.　イ In Mr. Davis's hometown, people wear yukata at the festival.　ウ Hana's father takes pictures at the festival every year.　エ Mr. Davis chose the festival for Hana's poster.

問9　あなたが外国の人に紹介したい日本の行事や文化は何ですか。次の書き出しに続けて、**2文以上**の英語で書きなさい。【4点】
I want to introduce ( ) to people from other countries.
[[lines:3]]
:::

:::a 解答・解説
問1　ア（直後にハナが「8月に開かれる」と答えている）
問2　a big festival（【2】最初の Mr. Davis の発言。「1月の大きな祭り」が家族連れに人気）
問3　ウ（ゆかたの話を受けて「すてきだね」→ 次の「いいちがいだと思う」につながる）
問4　I don't know which pictures to use.（疑問詞＋名詞＋to＋原形）
問5　（例）花火の写真とゆかたを着た人々の写真をのせること。／短い文で書くこと。（「日付・場所・花火について書く」はハナ自身の案なので不可）
問6　私は他の国の人々にそれ（この祭り）について知ってほしいです。
問7　ア（Of course. Come and show it to me anytime. につながる）
問8　ウ（【3】He takes a lot of pictures every year.）
問9　（例）I want to introduce *origami* to people from other countries. It is a traditional Japanese art of folding paper. We can make many things such as cranes and flowers with only one piece of paper.
:::

## 大問5形式の解き方（12点・8分）

:::box 手順
1. 英作文（3文以上）の**テーマを先に読む**。本文を読みながら「自分ならこう答える」を考える。
2. 本文の設問（2問）は、本文中の語句を使って答える。
3. 英作文は **「意見 → 理由 → 具体例（または一言）」の3文**。書き出しの文が与えられていたら必ず使う。
:::

## R5-1　大問5形式 演習1

:::passage 次の英文を読んで、あとの問いに答えなさい。
In my town, there is a small park called "Everyone's Garden." Ten years ago, it was just an empty place with a lot of trash. One day, an old woman living near the place started to grow flowers there. Her neighbors saw the flowers and began to help her. Now the garden has many kinds of flowers and vegetables, and about thirty people take care of it.

Every Sunday morning, the members meet at the garden. Children plant seeds, and elderly people teach them how to grow vegetables. In fall, they have a small festival and share the vegetables with the people in the town. The garden is not only a place for plants. It is also a place ( ① ) people can meet and talk.

The old woman says, "I just wanted to make the place beautiful. I didn't think it would become a place like this. Small actions can bring people together."
:::

:::q 問1　（①）に入る最も適切な語をア〜エから選びなさい。【3点】
ア which　イ where　ウ when　エ what

問2　この庭が「植物のための場所」であるだけでなく、どのような場所であると述べられていますか。日本語で書きなさい。【4点】

問3　あなたの町を、もっとよい場所にするために、あなたができることは何ですか。理由を含めて**3文以上**の英語で書きなさい。【5点】
[[lines:4]]
:::

:::a 解答・解説
問1　イ（a place where 人 can meet ＝ 人が会える場所。関係副詞 where は11月の範囲だが、「場所＋where」として選べるようにしておく）
問2　人々が集まって話をすることができる場所。
問3　（例）I can clean the park near my house with my friends. If the park is clean, more people will come and enjoy it. I also want to talk with elderly people in my town because they know a lot about its history.
**英作文の採点ポイント**：①3文以上あるか　②意見と理由がつながっているか　③三単現の s・時制・冠詞のミスがないか。
:::

## R5-3　大問5形式 演習3

:::passage 次の英文を読んで、あとの問いに答えなさい。
Reading picture books to children is a popular volunteer activity in Japan. My mother has done it at the city library for five years. Last month, I joined her for the first time.

I was nervous because I had to read in front of twenty children. Before the event, my mother gave me some advice. "Read slowly. Look at the children's faces. And enjoy the story yourself." I practiced at home many times.

On the day, I read a story about a little bear looking for his mother. When the bear found his mother at the end, a small girl in the front said, "I'm glad!" All the children smiled. At that moment, I understood why my mother has continued this activity for years. ( ① )

Now I read to the children every month. I still get nervous, but the children's smiles give me energy.
:::

:::q 問1　（①）に入る最も適切な文をア〜エから選びなさい。【3点】
ア She wanted to read many books herself.　イ Sharing a story makes both the reader and the listeners happy.　ウ The library is very quiet.　エ The children didn't listen to the story.

問2　筆者の母親が、イベントの前に与えた助言を日本語で2つ書きなさい。【4点】

問3　あなたがやってみたいボランティア活動は何ですか。理由を含めて**3文以上**の英語で書きなさい。【5点】
[[lines:4]]
:::

:::a 解答・解説
問1　イ（子どもたちの笑顔を見て「母が続ける理由がわかった」→ 物語を分かち合うことは読む人も聞く人も幸せにする）
問2　ゆっくり読むこと。／子どもたちの顔を見ること。（自分自身が物語を楽しむこと、も可）
問3　（例）I want to help elderly people in my town. Many of them live alone and feel lonely. I can visit them and talk with them, and I think they will be happy.
:::

## R5-4　大問5形式 演習4

:::passage 次の英文を読んで、あとの問いに答えなさい。
Last week, I went to a new restaurant near the station with my family. When we sat down, a robot came to our table and said, "Welcome! Please order on the screen." We touched the screen to choose our food. Ten minutes later, the same robot brought our dishes to our table.

My little brother was very excited and said, "The robot is cool!" My father said, "Robots are useful because restaurants don't have enough workers these days." But my mother said, "I like talking with the staff. ( ① )"

I thought about it after dinner. Robots can carry dishes, but they cannot understand our feelings. When I was sick last year, a kind waitress at another restaurant brought me warm soup without being asked. I still remember her smile.

I think robots and people should work together. Robots can do simple work, and people can do the things that only people can do.
:::

:::q 問1　（①）に入る最も適切な文をア〜エから選びなさい。【3点】
ア Robots are better than people.　イ The food was not delicious.　ウ A restaurant needs a warm heart, too.　エ I want to buy a robot.

問2　筆者は、ロボットにできないことは何だと述べていますか。日本語で書きなさい。【4点】

問3　あなたは、レストランでロボットが働くことについてどう思いますか。あなたの考えを、理由を含めて**3文以上**の英語で書きなさい。【5点】
[[lines:4]]
:::

:::a 解答・解説
問1　ウ（母は「店員と話すのが好き」→ 「レストランにはあたたかい心も必要」）
問2　私たちの気持ちを理解すること。
問3　（例）I think it is a good idea. Robots can carry dishes quickly, so the staff can spend more time talking with customers. But I hope people will keep working at restaurants because their kindness makes us happy.
:::

:::box RR（長文の再読）のやり方
- 間違えた大問3〜5を1本選び、**設問を見ずに本文だけを読んで、段落ごとに日本語で要約**（1段落1行）。
- そのあと、わからなかった単語に印をつけて、単語ノートへ。
- 最後に**音読1回**。長文は「知っている文章」を増やすほど読むのが速くなります。
:::
